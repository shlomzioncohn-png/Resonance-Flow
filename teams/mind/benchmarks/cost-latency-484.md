# Mind's cost and latency: the conclusion (#484)

> Over the #359 demo flow. The dataset is small (6 items, 2 runs) and from a laptop, so every
> number here is an order of magnitude, not a target.

Generated by `python -m mind.observability.report`; the figures below are from

```
python -m mind.observability.report \
    benchmarks/pipeline-baseline-482/20261027T094205Z-natural \
    benchmarks/pipeline-baseline-482/20261027T094907Z-slack-first
```

-- the #482 baseline's two 2026-10-27 runs, every stage recorded (`pipeline-baseline-482.md` has the
runs, the machine and their limits).

## The demo flow (6 items, 2 runs)

### The most expensive stage

It depends on the unit, and the two units are never added:

| Measure | Most expensive | Share | Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dollars** (LLM, at the published price) | `extraction` -- the only stage that costs dollars | 100% | -- |
| **CPU seconds** (local compute) | `to_text` -- ASR; OCR's CPU is not measured on Windows | 93% | `duplicate_check` 4%, `task_created` 2% |
| **Wall time in calls** | `to_text` | 38% | `score` 31% (degraded, see below), `extraction` 29% |

- **ASR is the stage to watch.** A meeting's ASR is ~31 CPU s (25.9 and 33.4) for 67.5 s of audio:
  23-30 CPU s per audio minute here, 17-31 in the long-job measurement
  (`serving-reliability-482.md`). Text and scan items cost under 1 CPU s each.
- **LLM extraction is the only dollar cost**, ~2-3 s and $0.0041-0.0050 a call ($0.0045 mean), one
  call per item.
- **`score`'s 31% is the demo's, not Mind's**: every score in these runs is degraded -- Chain is not
  running, and each failed lookup costs ~1.1-1.3 s (a DNS failure the 0.2 s timeout does not
  bound; reported to R3). The scoring model itself is ~2 ms.
- **Embedding and vector queries are cheap**: 0.1-0.3 s and up to ~1.2 CPU s an item.

### Cost per item

| Item type | Items | Dollars per item | CPU s per item | Span per item |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| audio (meeting) | 2 | $0.0049 | 30.7 | 16.6-18.3 s |
| scan (email + form) | 2 | $0.0044 | >=0.43 (OCR unmeasured) | 8.4-10.1 s |
| text (Slack) | 2 | $0.0042 | 0.70 | 6.6-8.4 s |
| **all** | 6 | **$0.0045 mean** | **>=10.6 mean** | median 9.3 s |

The dollar cost barely depends on the item's type (the LLM sees text either way); the CPU cost
depends on it entirely -- an audio item ~44x a text one here.

**10,000 items at this mix** (1/3 audio): ~$45 and at least 29.5 CPU-hours (a lower bound: OCR's
CPU is not measured). At a different mix the
CPU figure moves a lot: 10,000 meetings, ~85 CPU-hours; 10,000 text items, ~2. An estimate (the
mean times 10,000), not a measurement.

### Anything trending badly

Two runs per configuration are not a trend, so this is what the runs so far show, not a slope:

- **The API's memory was the one thing heading for a limit**: 453 MB idle against a 512Mi pod
  limit. Fixed in #482 -- 78 MB idle, 85 MB under load (`serving-reliability-482.md`).
- **The LLM's dollar cost will double on 2027-01-01**, when `gemini-3.6-flash`'s price does
  (`extraction/providers/pricing.py`): ~$0.009 an item at today's token counts.
- **ASR's latency is noisy, not growing**: the same 67.5 s recording took 6.9-29.3 s across four
  runs on this laptop. Nothing to read as a trend until runs are repeated on a steady machine.
- **ASR on a deployed landed runner** would be several times slower than here at the chart's
  default 500m CPU, and a 10-minute recording would pass the 240 s timeout (an estimate,
  `serving-reliability-482.md`). Not a trend -- a sizing decision for whoever deploys the runner.

## What this does not cover

- **Full scoring** -- every score here is degraded; it needs a live Chain.
- **The infrastructure's cost** -- Kafka, MinIO, Postgres, Inbox, the machine. No dollar figure here
  includes them, and CPU seconds are machine time, not a bill.
- **The cluster** -- every number is from one Windows laptop.
